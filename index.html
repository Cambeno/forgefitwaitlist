The Logger

Between sets is for resting. Not menus.

Built for one thumb, sweaty hands, and short windows between sets. Everything a serious log needs, nothing that slows you down.

  • Last session, right there. Every exercise shows your previous weight, reps and PR inline. Beat it.
  • One-tap sets. Empty set plus tick logs last session's numbers. Progressive overload in a single tap.
  • A rest timer that follows you. Fires on your lock screen, and you can log the next set straight from the notification.
  • Plate math, done. Type a weight, see the per-side plate breakdown instantly.
  • Lifter-grade detail. RPE tags, warmup, drop and failure sets, supersets, custom exercises.

Ember, your AI coach

A coach that has actually read your training log.

Ember isn't a chatbot with a six-pack avatar. It knows your history, your PRs and your plan, and it programs today's session from what your body did this week.

  • Ask anything. Straight answers built on your own numbers, not generic templates.
  • Full workouts in seconds. Generated to your goal, your equipment and how recovered you are today.
  • Program critique. Paste any program and get told what works, what's broken and what to change.

Recovery-driven training

Go heavy when you're primed. Back off when you're not.

Connect Apple Health and ForgeFit reads your sleep, HRV and resting heart rate into one readiness score, then tells you how hard today should be. Your AI-generated sessions scale with it automatically.

  • Recovery, strain and heat at a glance, every morning.
  • No junk volume. Under-recovered days get lighter loads and smarter work, automatically.
  • Private by design. Health data is read-only, never sold, never used for ads.
